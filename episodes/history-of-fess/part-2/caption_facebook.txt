How did one sinus operation conquer the world? 🌍
Part 2 of 5 in our History of FESS series.

After its birth in Graz and Baltimore, functional endoscopic sinus surgery spread through four channels:

1️⃣ Societies — the American Rhinologic Society (1954), the European (1963, Leiden), the International (1965, Kyoto) and ISIAN (1976, Tokyo), followed by national societies.
2️⃣ Courses — Johns Hopkins from 1985, Graz from 1986, then surgeons from every continent.
3️⃣ Tools — computer navigation (first used in 1987), powered instruments (1994), balloon dilation (2006).
4️⃣ A common language — CT and endoscopic scores (1993, 1995), standard smell tests, the SNOT-22 questionnaire validated on 3,128 patients (2009), and consensus papers on sinusitis, tumours, anatomy and smell.

But access is not universal: in 15 sub-Saharan countries, ENT surgeons grew by 43% between 2009 and 2015, while the population grew by 23%. The first barrier is basic equipment.
