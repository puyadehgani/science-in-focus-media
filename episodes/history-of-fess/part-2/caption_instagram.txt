How did one sinus operation conquer the world? 🌍 Part 2/5 · History of FESS

From a few centres to every continent:
• Societies: American Rhinologic Society 1954 · European 1963 (Leiden) · International 1965 (Kyoto) · ISIAN 1976 (Tokyo)
• Courses: Johns Hopkins from 1985, Graz from 1986
• Tools: navigation 1987 · powered instruments 1994 · balloon dilation 2006
• A common language: CT score 1993 · endoscopic score 1995 · SNOT-22 (3,128 patients, 2009) · EPOS and consensus papers
• Access is not universal: in 15 sub-Saharan countries ENT surgeons +43% (2009–2015), population +23%. First barrier: basic equipment.
